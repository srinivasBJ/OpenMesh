# Code Of Conduct

OpenMeshAI is an open-source project for people building and studying agent systems. We want the community to be rigorous, welcoming, and safe for contributors at different experience levels.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that helps the project:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about technical concerns.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Credit ideas and contributions.
- Make space for new contributors to ask basic questions.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Dismissing contributors based on identity, background, or experience level.
- Pressuring maintainers or contributors for unpaid urgency.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block users, or take other reasonable action when behavior harms the community.

If you need to report a concern, contact the maintainers privately once a public contact method is published. Until then, avoid escalating sensitive issues in public threads unless immediate visibility is necessary for safety.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, community spaces, and project-related events.

## Attribution
